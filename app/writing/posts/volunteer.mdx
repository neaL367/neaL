Outside of code, I volunteer as an IT speaker for programs that teach beginners and underprivileged students.

The first time I explained a loop to a room of students who had never written one, I realised how much of my own understanding was held together by habit rather than by actual reasoning. **Teaching forces you to find the gaps.**

Most of what I do is demystify: what a computer actually does, why a program fails, and how to get unstuck without someone handing you the answer. I do not try to turn everyone into a developer. I try to leave students with **the confidence to open a terminal**, which is usually the real barrier.
