A short overview of Rockstar Games and why its catalogue keeps breaking records. All figures below are as of September 2026. Ask Nara about any name here for the full entry.

## Grand Theft Auto VI

Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled for 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, after delays from a 2025 window to 26 May 2026. No PC version is scheduled at launch. It returns to Vice City in the Florida-like state of Leonida, following dual protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos — the series' first female lead of the modern era — framed as a Bonnie-and-Clyde couple. The standard edition costs $79.99 with a $100 Ultimate Edition; pre-orders opened 25 June 2026 and most buyers chose Ultimate. Trailer 2 drew a record 475 million views in 24 hours.

## GTA V sales

GTA V has sold in over 230 million units as of August 2026, with the GTA franchise near 475 million lifetime. Red Dead Redemption 2 adds over 87 million, making it the third best-selling game ever behind Minecraft and GTA V. Take-Two guides $8 to $8.2 billion in net bookings for fiscal 2027, driven by the GTA VI launch. The same game selling across three console generations plus a decade of GTA Online revenue is the industry outlier everything else is measured against.

## Rockstar Games and Take-Two

Rockstar Games was founded in December 1998 as a Take-Two Interactive label, built from BMG Interactive assets by Sam Houser, Dan Houser, Terry Donovan, Jamie King and Gary Foreman. Sam Houser remains president; Dan Houser left in March 2020 and founded Absurd Ventures in 2023. Take-Two chairman and CEO Strauss Zelnick runs the parent company, which also owns 2K and bought Zynga for about $12.7 billion in 2022. Flagship development sits at Rockstar North in Edinburgh with Rockstar San Diego, Toronto, Leeds, Lincoln, Dundee and newer support studios.

## The RAGE engine

RAGE is Rockstar's in-house game engine, first used on Table Tennis in 2006 and the foundation of every major title since, from GTA IV through Red Dead Redemption 2. It layers licensed Euphoria animation over its own simulation, streams open worlds without loading screens, and reached 60fps performance modes with raytracing in the 2022 Expanded and Enhanced upgrade of GTA V. RenderWare powered the PlayStation 2 era before EA bought Criterion in 2004.

## Red Dead Redemption

Red Dead Redemption (2010) follows John Marston in 1911, forced by federal agents to hunt his old Van der Linde gang across New Austin, Nuevo Paraíso and West Elizabeth. Its 2018 prequel Red Dead Redemption 2 follows Arthur Morgan and carries about 500,000 lines of dialogue with 2,200 days of motion capture. The Undead Nightmare expansion retells the frontier as zombie horror. Red Dead Online wound down major updates after 2021 as Rockstar focused on GTA VI.

## L.A. Noire

L.A. Noire (2011) is the 1947 LAPD detective game from Team Bondi, published by Rockstar after a seven-year development. Officer Cole Phelps works five desks of cases across a recreated Los Angeles, interrogating suspects through MotionScan face capture. Crunch and crediting controversies ended the partnership; Team Bondi entered liquidation that October. Founder Brendan McNamara later studio became Rockstar Australia in 2025.
